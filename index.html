Born in Kerala kitchens, to end knife fatigue, scrap waste and the dirty market run.

Prep D started from one stubborn idea: nobody should spend 45 minutes chopping, peeling shallots and scraping a slimy board just to eat a decent home-cooked meal.

We buy direct from partner farms following good agricultural practice in Wayanad, Palakkad and the Nilgiris. Vegetables reach our cold prep facility early in the morning, go through triple ozonated water washing, uniform cut calibration and vacuum sealing — all inside 90 minutes.

What arrives at your door is 100% edible, sanitised, nutrient-intact food, ready for the pan the moment the pouch opens.

Mission

To make clean home cooking effortless, affordable and completely scrap-free — for every busy family and every professional kitchen in South India.

Vision

To build a trusted cold-chain farm-to-pan network that takes thousands of tons of kitchen waste out of the system while making daily nutrition genuinely easy.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PREP'D hygiene standards, doorstep cold dispatch, and shelf life.

Where does PREP'D deliver, and what are the delivery timings?

We currently deliver across all major localities in Malappuram district (Manjeri, Malappuram Town, Perinthalmanna, Tirur, Kottakkal) and Kozhikode (Calicut City, Chevayur, Palayam, Mavoor Road, Feroke). We offer two daily slots: Morning (6:30 AM – 8:30 AM) and Evening (4:30 PM – 7:00 PM).

How are vegetables kept fresh during transit?

All our packs are sealed in vacuum or modified-atmosphere packaging and dispatched from a temperature-controlled facility. Insulated delivery bags maintain the cold chain from our facility all the way to your door.

How should I store PREP'D packs when they arrive?

Place packs in the refrigerator (2 °C – 6 °C) immediately on arrival. Keep them sealed until use. Do not store near raw meat or strong-smelling foods. Once opened, use within 24 hours.

What is the shelf life of PREP'D products?

Fresh-cut vegetable packs stay fresh for 2–3 days when refrigerated. Vacuum-sealed and modified-atmosphere packs last up to 5 days. Power Breakfast mixes have a shelf life of 3–4 days. Always check the best-before date printed on each pack.

What is your freshness guarantee and return policy?

Every pack leaves our facility fresh-inspected. If your order arrives damaged, spoiled, or incorrect, WhatsApp us within 2 hours of delivery with a photo and we will arrange a replacement or full refund — no questions asked.

Do I need to wash the vegetables again before putting them in my pan?

PREP'D vegetables are triple-washed and hygienically cut at our facility before packing. You can cook them straight from the pack. A quick rinse is optional but not required.

Are any preservatives, chemicals, or artificial colorings added?

Absolutely not. PREP'D products contain only the vegetable or ingredient listed — nothing else. Freshness is maintained purely through cold-chain management and hygienic processing, not chemicals.
